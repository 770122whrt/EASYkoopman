# v1.0 Milestone Audit

## Verdict

**Gaps found, accepted at close.** The user explicitly approved closing v1.0 at Phase 5.4 on 2026-08-09 and carrying the unresolved items as technical debt instead of extending the milestone through Phase 5.5/5.6.

This verdict separates two facts:

1. The end-to-end research system is runnable and has local plus Isaac evidence.
2. The historical GSD requirement-verification documents are incomplete, and the final parameter search did not produce a promotable controller.

## Accepted Gaps

- No candidate has proved broad superiority over legacy/S-Surface.
- Phase 5.4 ended with `selection_status=no_selection`.
- Adapter/reference semantics and fallback cost logic remain unresolved.
- Evidence covers one EasyUUV configuration only.
- No online adaptation, LLM runtime, Sim2Real or real-hardware evidence exists.
- Nine phase directories lack a formal `VERIFICATION.md`; Phase 3.5 also lacks a summary.

## Closure Decision

The milestone is suitable to close as a **single-configuration research baseline** because its end-to-end chain is runnable, tested and reproducible enough to serve as the starting point for later work. It is not suitable to close under a claim of controller superiority or deployment readiness.
