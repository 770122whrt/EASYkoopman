## Interpretation

The user-requested `reward_v1 + adapter_soft` cross did not help. It eliminated policy action clipping, but worsened PWM saturation, fallback and depth RMSE.

The strongest signal is `reward_v1 + mpc_health`. It improved PWM saturation, clipping, depth RMSE and attitude RMSE, but fallback rose too much. This makes it a useful diagnostic anchor, not a selected controller.

## Next Recommended Phase

Plan Phase 5.4 around fallback-aware MPC/reward diagnosis:

- Preserve the PWM/depth gains seen in `cross_reward_v1_mpc_health`.
- Reduce fallback rate, especially timeout-driven fallback.
- Add reason-aware fallback analysis before further PPO or LLM work.
- Do not promote LLM control until the PPO+Koopman-MPC layer has a stable selected profile.
