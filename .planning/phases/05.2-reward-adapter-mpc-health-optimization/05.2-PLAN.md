## Wave 5: Local Validators And Selection Gate

Likely files:

- Create: `workflows/validate_phase5_2_health_summary.py`

- Test: `tests/test_phase5_2_health_validator.py`

Validation hard failures:

```text
missing checkpoint
invalid provenance
non-finite observation/action/reward/PWM
PWM outside [-1, 1]
missing adapter refresh
missing profile_id or changed_axis
matched eval missing for selected candidate
```

Promotion rule:

```text
Pass hard gates
and improve the primary health metric mean across trajectories by at least 20 percent
and meet the minimum absolute improvement for that metric.
```

Minimum absolute improvements:

```text
pwm_saturation_rate: >= 0.05 absolute
fallback_rate: >= 0.015 absolute if baseline >= 0.05
policy_action_clip_rate_mean: >= 0.03 absolute
```

Regression guards:

```text
fallback_rate:
  no trajectory may have both absolute increase > 0.02 and relative increase > 25%

depth_rmse:
  mean depth RMSE may not worsen by more than 10%

attitude_rmse / quaternion_error:
  mean attitude RMSE may not worsen by more than 10%

matched evaluation:
  step, sine and irregular must complete for promotion; partial runs are diagnostic only
```

Acceptance:

- Selection produces `source/results/koopman_phase5_2/selected_health_candidate_manifest.json`.
- Manifest records `selected_reason`, `improved_metric`, `baseline_value`, `candidate_value` and regression checks.
- If no candidate passes, report `no_selection` with bottleneck classification.

## Review Feedback Incorporated

The Phase 5.2 review changed the executable contract:

1. `reward_v1_only` uses `w_fallback=0.30`; reason-aware fallback penalty is allowed but not required.
2. `adapter_soft_v1_only` uses `rpy_delta_scale=0.30` and `depth_delta_scale=0.40`.
3. `mpc_health_v1_only` keeps `mpc_delta_pwm_limit=0.35` and changes only control/smoothness weights.
4. `mpc_delta_pwm_limit=0.25` becomes optional `mpc_delta_limit_probe_v1`, not initial `mpc_health_v1`.
5. `baseline_rerun` is mandatory.
6. Promotion uses 20 percent relative improvement plus absolute improvement and depth/attitude/fallback regression guards.
7. Combined profile is deferred to Phase 5.2b or Phase 5.3.
