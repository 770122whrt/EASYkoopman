2. Run each formal LOCO fold in a fresh Python process; verify every durable fold sealed source state and froze primary/expert before test authorization. A failed process may be rerun only for its missing fold output; completed validated fold outputs are not recomputed.

4. Stop immediately on any protocol deviation; do not alter candidates, gates or thresholds.
